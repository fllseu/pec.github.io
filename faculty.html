Prof. Lingling Fan

Director | PhD in Electrical Engineering | IEEE Fellow
Focus: Dr. Fan's research interests include control, dynamics, optimization, and system identification of power systems, power electronics and electric machines. Her recent research projects focus on mechanism analysis of various dynamics seen in the real world (wind farm oscillations, solar PV tripping), electromagnetic transient simulation, inverter-based resource dynamic model identification through data-driven methods, and power electronic converter control design. In 2022, she was elevated to IEEE Fellow for her contributions to stability analysis and control of inverter-based resources.

Prof. Zhixin Miao

Associate Director | PhD in Electrical Engineering | Professional Engineer
Focus: Power system protection, power system stability, hardware control prototyping, and hardware-in-the-loop testbed design. Dr. Miao serves as an associate editor for IEEE trans. Sustainable Energy.
